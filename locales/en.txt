37=May 3, 1991
38=Born in London
39=He is the eldest son of Andrea Acutis and Antonia Salzano, both Italian, who were living in England for work. Shortly afterwards, the family settles in Milan.
40=September 1997
41=Early school years
42=He begins primary school at the San Carlo Institute in Milan. From an early age, he asks questions about faith and finds answers with the help of those who care for him.
43=At age 7
44=First Communion
45=He receives it at the monastery of Bernaga. From then on, the Eucharist becomes the center of his life and he attends Mass every day.
46=Around age 11
47=A great idea is born
48=He begins researching Eucharistic miracles throughout history. For about two and a half years, he prepares an exhibition first presented in 2005.
49=October 2006
50=Leukemia and death
51=He is diagnosed with rapidly progressing leukemia. He offers his suffering for the Pope and the Church and dies on October 12, 2006, at age 15.
52=A simple faith,
53=every day
54=He did not do extraordinary things: he did everyday things with extraordinary love.
55=The Eucharist
56=He called it his “highway to Heaven.” He went to Mass daily and spent time before the Blessed Sacrament.
57=Confession and the rosary
58=He went to confession regularly and prayed the rosary. He visited Lourdes and Fátima on pilgrimage.
59=Serving others
60=He helped classmates in need, volunteered and reached out to those who were alone.

65=The cyber-apostle
66=of the Eucharist
67=He taught himself programming and web design. With these skills, he created a website for his parish and, above all, a virtual exhibition bringing together Eucharistic miracles from twenty countries.
68=The exhibition has around 160 panels, is translated into several languages and is displayed in parishes and shrines around the world. It remains available online.

100=First miracle · Brazil
103=Approved for beatification
104=Matheus Vianna was born with an annular pancreas, a malformation that caused constant vomiting and weight loss. On October 12, 2013, in a chapel displaying a relic of Carlo, he ate a full meal and never vomited again.
105=Second miracle · Costa Rica
107=Florence, Italy · 2022
108=Approved for canonization
109=Valeria Valverde, aged 21, suffered a severe head injury in a bicycle accident. Her mother prayed at Carlo’s tomb in Assisi, and that same day Valeria began breathing on her own again. Two months later, she traveled to Assisi to give thanks.
110=A connection with Brazil
111=October 12,
112=twice
113=Carlo died on October 12, 2006, the feast of Our Lady of Aparecida, patroness of Brazil. It was also on October 12, seven years later, that Matheus was healed in Campo Grande.

179=questions
180=Why is he called the first millennial saint?
181=Born in 1991, he belongs to the millennial generation and is the first saint from that generation recognized by the Catholic Church.
182=Why is he a patron of the internet?
183=He used programming and web design to document Eucharistic miracles and share his faith. Many Catholics consider him their patron, although it is not an official title.
184=What did he die of?
185=Rapidly progressing leukemia, in October 2006, at age 15. He offered his suffering for the Pope and the Church.
186=Where is his tomb?
187=In the Sanctuary of the Spoliation in Assisi, Italy, where his body rests and can be visited.
188=When is his feast celebrated?
189=His memorial is celebrated on October 12, the anniversary of his death.
